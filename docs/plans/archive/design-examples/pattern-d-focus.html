Paragraphs ／ Should students wear school uniforms?　4. 下書き・推敲書き出し

OUTLINE «

  • 制服は義務にすべきではない
  • 個性を表現できない
  • 自分で服を選ぶと自分らしくいられる
  • お金がかかる
  • 一式 5 万円以上、成長で買い替え
  • 季節に合わない
  • 夏は暑く、冬は寒い
  • 結論: 服は自分で選べるほうがよい
条件　120–150 語・8–10 文 ・ 理由を 3 つ挙げ First / Second / Finally を使う ・ 結論で topic sentence を言い換える
In my opinion, students should not have to wear school uniforms. First, uniforms take away a chance to show personality. For example, I feel more like myself when I choose my own clothes in the morning. Second, uniforms are expensive for families. A full set often costs more than 50,000 yen, and growing students need new ones every few years. Finally, uniforms are not comfortable in every season.

Opinion paragraph

1

Topic sentence意見

段落の最初の 1 文。テーマに対する自分の意見をはっきり書く。「賛成か反対か」が読み手にすぐ分かるようにする。

2

Reasons理由

意見を支える理由を 2〜3 つ。First, / Second, / Finally, などの transition words で始める。

3

Examples / Details例・詳細

理由ごとに、具体的な例・数字・体験を 1〜2 文添える。理由だけでは説得力が弱い。

4

Concluding sentence結論

最後の 1 文。topic sentence の意見を別の言葉で言い換えて締める。新しい理由は出さない。

I believe that high school students should have part-time jobs. First, working teaches students how to manage money. For example, when they earn their own money, they start to think carefully before they buy something. Second, a part-time job helps students learn social skills. At work, they talk with customers and coworkers of different ages, so they learn how to communicate politely. Finally, students can discover what kind of work they enjoy. This experience can help them choose a future career. For these reasons, I think that having a part-time job is a valuable experience for high school students.
